fPaint is a free, open-source painting and drawing app with the tools you expect from a professional studio, and nothing in the way: no ads, no accounts, no subscriptions, no tracking.

BRUSHES AND EFFECTS
• Pencil, brush and eraser with pressure sensitivity for stylus and pen
• Smudge to blend colors the way you would with your finger
• Effects you paint on like any brush: blur, sharpen, brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, grayscale, noise, pixelate, shadow and vignette
• Lines, rectangles, circles and text

LAYERS
• As many layers as you need, with blend modes, opacity, visibility and locking
• Rename, reorder and merge
• Live thumbnails of every layer

SELECTIONS AND TRANSFORM
• Rectangle, ellipse, lasso, magic wand and a selection brush
• Add to and subtract from any selection
• Move, scale, rotate and reshape selected content; copy, cut, paste and duplicate
• Every brush and effect stays inside the active selection

COLOR AND FILL
• Color wheel, palettes, recent colors and an eyedropper
• Solid, linear gradient and radial gradient fills with a live preview

A CANVAS THAT KEEPS UP
• Pinch to zoom, pan and rotate the view freely, then snap back upright
• A radial quick menu with your recent tools, colors and brush size
• Full undo and redo, so you can experiment
• Automatic recovery of unsaved work if the app is closed

YOUR FILES, YOUR FORMATS
• Open PNG, JPEG, TIFF, WEBP, HEIC, OpenRaster (ORA) and Photoshop (PSD) files
• Save layered work as ORA or TIFF and export to PNG, JPEG, TIFF and WEBP
• Edit files in place from the Files app and cloud drives

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
fPaint works offline and collects no data at all. Your artwork never leaves your device unless you share it.

OPEN SOURCE
fPaint is MIT-licensed and built in the open. Read the code, report an issue or contribute at github.com/vteam-com/fPaint.
